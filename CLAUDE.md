# This folder is the hatchery kit

Not the owner's repo — the kit they unpacked. `START-HERE.md` and `LETTER.md` are for them; everything
else is in `kit/` (the plugin, the repo skeleton `kit/template/`, the setup, the reader documents).

- If the owner asks to set up ("set me up"), says "continue setup", or this is plainly their first
  session here: follow [`kit/SETUP.md`](kit/SETUP.md), step by step. Paths in it are relative to `kit/`.
- Do not edit files in this folder; a newer kit is unpacked over it (`kit/WALKTHROUGH.md` § Later, "A
  newer kit"). Their own work lives in the repo `kit/SETUP.md` step 2 creates.
- Once setup is done, point them to their repo folder and `/hatchery:overlord`.
